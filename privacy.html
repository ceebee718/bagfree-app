Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

BagFree is operated by Batch Physics Consulting, LLC ("BagFree," "we," "us," or "our"), and this Privacy Policy covers the BagFree website and mobile application (together, the "Service"), a luggage‑free travel platform that lets travelers reserve curated apparel and travel-essential bundles for delivery to their destination. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Service.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by using the Service, and limited information from third parties who help us operate it.

  • Account information: name, email address, and phone number when you create an account or complete a reservation.
  • Order and preference information: the bundles, quantities, and sizes you order; your delivery city, hotel, and travel dates; and any size preference we save to speed up future checkouts.
  • Payment information: when you make a purchase, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers — Stripe handles that in accordance with its own privacy policy and PCI‑compliance standards.
  • Location information: if you use map or "nearby" features, we may request access to your device's location to show you partner locations and directions near you. This is only used while you're actively using that feature and is never sold.
  • Communications: messages you send through our concierge chat or support channels, including messages handled by an AI-assisted concierge tool.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as device type, browser, and how you interact with the Service, used to keep it working reliably and improve it over time.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and to process and fulfill your orders.
  • To communicate with you about your reservations, orders, and account (confirmations, receipts, and support).
  • To personalize your experience — for example, pre‑filling your preferred size or city.
  • To operate loyalty and rewards features (such as BAG Points) tied to your account.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service and prevent fraud.
  • To improve the Service based on how it's actually used.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that make BagFree work, and only as needed for them to perform their function:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account, order, and profile data.
  • Netlify — website and application hosting.
  • Email and messaging providers we use to send confirmations, receipts, and respond to support requests.
  • Hotel and local partner businesses, limited to what's needed to fulfill and deliver your order (e.g., your name and travel dates).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of BagFree, our users, or the public.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see "Your Choices" below.

Your Choices

  • Access & correction: you can review and update your account information at any time from your account settings.
  • Deletion: you can request that we delete your account and personal data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Location: you can disable location access at any time through your device's settings; some map features may not work without it.
  • Marketing communications: you can opt out of promotional emails using the unsubscribe link included in those emails. You'll still receive transactional messages related to active orders.
If you are located in the EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and the right to opt out of certain data sharing. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
