Every partner plays a part in helping travelers arrive prepared, experience more, and leave less behind.

Curator

Share local knowledge, create guides, recommend experiences, and earn rewards for every traveler you help.

Experience Host

Offer tours, classes, activities, and authentic local experiences to BagFree travelers.

Delivery Partner

Help deliver clothing bundles, travel essentials, meals, and future BagFree services to hotels.

Restaurant Partner

Offer featured dining experiences and traveler promotions through the BagFree platform.

Business Partner

Sustainability Partner
