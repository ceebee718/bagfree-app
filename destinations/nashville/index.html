Nashville.

Louder than you planned. Worth every minute.

Nashville operates on a frequency you feel before you hear it.

By noon on a Friday, Broadway is already going. By midnight, you'll understand why people plan to stay one night and book three more. The honky-tonks are only part of it — the East Nashville restaurant scene, the 12South boutiques, the rooftop bars that know exactly what they're doing.

You will stay longer than you planned. Pack accordingly. We'll have the rest waiting.

Where to go
Four places worth knowing.
Fine Dining
The Catbird Seat
16-seat chef's counter, tasting menu only, months-long reservation list. Worth every effort.
Cocktails
Pinewood Social
Bowling lanes, a pool, craft cocktails, excellent food. Somehow not trying too hard.
Neighborhood
12South
Boutiques, brunch spots, and the Imogene + Willie denim store. One of the best shopping streets in the South.
Brunch
Biscuit Love
The Geraldine biscuit. Full stop. Arrive at 8am or accept a wait.
