Miami.

Bright at midnight. Alive at noon. Never one thing.

Scroll to explore
Miami gives you three cities depending on when you arrive. Each one demands a different wardrobe.

The dawn city is quiet and pink, the light on Biscayne Bay doing things that don't photograph correctly. The afternoon city is blinding — the kind of sun that makes every color look saturated beyond belief. The midnight city is its own geography entirely, operating by rules that were invented sometime around 2019 and haven't been written down.

You'll need something for each. We've thought about it so you don't have to. Your arrival kit is already at the hotel.
