How It Works
Three steps. Zero waste.
1
Donate
Heading home? Leave quality items you don’t want to pack with BagFree. Earn credit back on your order or BAG Points as a reward.
2
Curate
We verify condition, photograph each item, and list it in the Savannah marketplace with full transparency.
3
Redeem
Another traveler redeems BAG Points to claim items. Delivered to their Savannah hotel — delivery included in the BAG price.

Earn BAG

Earn With Every Delivery
BAG Points for Delivery Crews
You don’t have to be a traveler to earn BAG. Our delivery crew earns points based on the quality of every delivery — because how an item arrives matters as much as the item itself.
On-Time Delivery
Deliver within the promised window and earn base BAG Points on every order. Consistency builds your score.
Traveler Ratings
Travelers rate every delivery. 5-star ratings earn bonus BAG. Maintain a 4.8+ average and unlock Elite Driver status.
Item Condition
Items arrive in the same condition they left. Careful handling earns quality bonuses — especially for Second Journey items.
Spend Anywhere
Use your BAG Points to claim Second Journey items, gift them to travelers, or redeem through the BagFree marketplace.

Partner With Us
Businesses Can Help Travelers Leave Less Behind
Hotels, restaurants, vendors, and local partners play a critical role in sustainable travel. Second Journey™ gives businesses a branded way to contribute — and be recognized for it.
Cash Support
Fund the logistics behind Second Journey — storage, cleaning, quality verification, and delivery of redistributed items to travelers who need them.
Clothing Contributions
Quality apparel from hotel lost-and-found, retail overstock, or event surplus gets a second life — helping travelers who arrive unprepared or face unexpected weather.
Travel Essentials
Unopened toiletries, sealed chargers, and unused travel accessories reduce waste and keep travelers equipped — without producing new inventory.
Storage Space
Businesses with available space can host a Second Journey collection point — expanding the network and making it easier for travelers to contribute items.
