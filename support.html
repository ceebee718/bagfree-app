Orders & Delivery

How does BagFree delivery work?

You choose a curated bundle, your city, hotel, and travel dates at checkout. We coordinate delivery so your items are ready at your hotel — no packing, no checked bag.

Can I change my size or cancel an item after ordering?

Email us as soon as possible at larry@bagfree.app with your order details. We can usually adjust size or quantity if your order hasn't shipped yet.

Where can I see my past orders?

Your order history is on your Dashboard under "Recent Orders," including the items, sizes, and status of each trip.

Account & Preferences

Does BagFree remember my size?

Yes — after your first order, we save your size to your profile so it's pre-selected the next time you check out. You can change it anytime from your Dashboard.

How do I delete my account or personal data?

Email larry@bagfree.app and we'll process your deletion request. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how long we keep it.

Passport & Rewards

What is the Passport feature?

Your Passport tracks real places you visit in each BagFree city — check in at partner locations to collect stamps and earn BAG Points.

How do BAG Points work?

You earn points from purchases and passport stamps. Points and membership details are available on your Rewards page.

Payments

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe — BagFree never stores your full card number on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
