Where to go
Four places worth knowing.
Brunch
Balthazar
Soho brasserie, marble floors, the omelette you'll think about on the flight home. Arrive before 10am or wait.
Walk
The High Line
Elevated rail park from Meatpacking to Hudson Yards. Best on a Tuesday morning before the tourists find it.
Deli
Russ & Daughters Cafe
Lower East Side, since 1914. The proper bagel with proper lox. Non-negotiable on any New York visit.
Cocktails
The NoMad Bar
Ornate, unhurried, exceptional cocktails. The kind of bar that makes you feel like you made the right choice coming here.
