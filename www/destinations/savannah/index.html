Savannah.

Slower. More intentional. Just as you should be.

Scroll to explore
Savannah doesn't perform for you. The squares are always there. The moss is always low.

The light here does something in the late afternoon — filters through the live oaks and lands on the cobblestones at exactly the angle that makes you stop. You'll need less than you think you will. A few good pieces, comfortable shoes, nowhere urgent to be.

This is a city that rewards slowness. The best table at the Olde Pink House isn't the one by the window — it's the one you didn't rush to. Pack accordingly. We'll have the rest waiting.
