The process

From application to first rental.

Four steps. Typically four to six weeks from application to launch.

i.
Apply
Tell us your city, your aesthetic, your background. Two paragraphs, no deck required. We read every application personally and respond within one week.
ii.
Meet the team
A conversation with Selena's creative team to align on aesthetic, city strategy, and hotel relationships. We're selective because every curator represents the full brand.
iii.
Build your edit
We fund your starter wardrobe — $3,000–5,000 of curated pieces selected together. You add your own where the vision calls for it. Every piece is photographed for your profile.
iv.
Launch and earn
Your profile goes live on The Nuor Edit. Hotel guests book directly through BagFree. You receive 75–85% of every rental. The platform handles payment, scheduling, and concierge outreach.
